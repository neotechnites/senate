# PANOPTICON reveal trailer: CC music shortlist (2026-10-05)

Reference: "SR20DET" by Blksmiith (hard start, drums, cut, bass drop at about 5 s).

**Method.** I swept every Free Music Archive track tagged Jungle, Drum & Bass, Breakcore-Hard or Breakbeat (2,135 tracks) and read the licence on each track page. 223 allowed commercial use. I dropped anything NC, ND or flagged "AI generated? Yes" by FMA. I also dropped uploaders whose catalogues read as stock or AI (Alex Morgan, Adeline Yeo, Pamela Yuen, Atrahasis). I then analysed the first 16 s of each remaining track by computer for energy, sub-bass and hi-hat content. The same was done for Internet Archive CC BY netlabel releases (on-mix, RusZud/Wicked Area) and a ccMixter API pass. ccMixter had nothing usable: full DnB mixes there are NC or loop packs.

**The first-15 s descriptions come from that analysis, not from listening.** Confirm by ear. Vocals are only confirmed absent where FMA marks the track "Instrumental: Yes".

**Downloads.** All 12 are in `~/Desktop/panopticon-renders/trailer_reveal/music_audition_cc/`. The FMA "Download" button needs a login, so the FMA files come from the public stream the licence page's player serves (the full track). The Internet Archive files are direct downloads.

**Licence notes for all tracks.**
- **CC BY:** credit the artist and title, link the licence, and say if you edited the track (for example "edited").
- **CC BY-SA:** putting music under a video counts as an adaptation, so the trailer itself would have to be released under CC BY-SA. Avoid it unless you accept that.
- **Under 4.0:** a lapse in attribution can be fixed within 30 days.

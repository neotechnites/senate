# SoundCloud artists with commercial-use CC jungle / DnB (2026-10-05)

Method: SoundCloud api-v2 search with `filter.license=to_use_commercially` / `to_modify_commercially` across ~150 queries and genre-tag filters (jungle, drum & bass, dnb, liquid, breakbeat, breaks, amen, atmospheric, drumfunk, and many "jungle/dnb + word" combinations, plus date and duration filters). That turned up about 4,500 uploaders. I pulled the full track list of each one with 8 or more tracks (~3,300) and counted licences per track. **Commercially usable = CC BY, CC BY-SA, or CC0/no-rights-reserved.** I left out -NC and -ND.

**Originals** = tracks whose genre, tag or title matches jungle/DnB/breaks and whose title does not say remix, bootleg, edit, mix, set, cover or "vs". Excluded: arcologies and everyone in `music_artists` or in the earlier music_*.md files; labels and aggregators (Metalheadz, NeuroDNB, Close 2 Death, NICE UP!, FINRG and similar, whose CC tags are almost certainly a default setting and not the artist's choice); DJ-mix accounts; bootleg/remix-heavy accounts (Dotronix, IRONIXX, Xsylum, MattyBlades, SubDocta and others); AI/library uploaders (NCMFYT, Alex-Productions, LogSquare and others). All profile and track links returned HTTP 200 from SoundCloud oEmbed.

**Caveats (read before using anything):**
- The licence on SoundCloud is set by the uploader and nobody checks it. Several of these tracks were also released on labels ("OUT NOW on X", "Forthcoming on Y"). For those, the label may hold the rights, so the CC tag may not be valid. Lowest risk: tracks with no label mention, from artists whose bio says the music is free to use (Staytment, canton, Fiona Preroll).
- **CC BY-SA in a trailer:** CC 4.0 treats music synced to video as an adaptation, so ShareAlike would in principle apply to the trailer video itself. **CC BY only needs a credit line.** Prefer CC BY tracks for the trailer.
- I did not listen to any of this. Genre comes from the artists' own tags and titles. None of these artists is a "Jumper"-style song that lots of games already use, and I did not check whether any games use them.
- The pool is thin. Most CC-commercial "jungle" on SoundCloud is labels, DJ mixes and bootlegs. Only about 15 individual producers made it through.

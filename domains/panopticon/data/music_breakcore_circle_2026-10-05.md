# PANOPTICON music: the Blksmiith "chill breakcore" circle (2026-10-05)

Method: the allevents.in page for Blksmiith lists exactly six "fans also like" artists (MIMIDEATH, Acidgvrl, Purity Filter, AKIBA, usedcvnt, KENTENSHI). One hop out came from the tracklists of five YouTube atmospheric breakcore/jungle mixes plus a web search. 40+ names seen, 24 artists had tracks downloaded (YouTube audio, private evaluation only, kept outside any git repo) and measured.

Measuring: tempo by comb filter on the onset envelope (reference "SR20DET" measured 169.2 bpm, true about 170, so +/-2 bpm). "Drop" = biggest loudness jump after a quieter 4 s passage, refined to 20 ms. Dark/minor is a crude chroma key estimate, not a listening judgement. Nobody was contacted. None of these artists publishes a licensing or commission statement on the pages I opened.

Followers: YouTube subscriber counts from yt-dlp. SoundCloud counts from the public profile HTML (slug guessed, so it could be a namesake; flagged where doubtful). Spotify listeners were not readable. Everyone kept is far below Blksmiith (about 319k).

Audition videos: ~/Desktop/panopticon-renders/trailer_reveal/music_audition_breakcore/ (picture from rough_v24, drop at 5.70 s, 43.5 s, about -13 LUFS integrated, 2 s fade-out).

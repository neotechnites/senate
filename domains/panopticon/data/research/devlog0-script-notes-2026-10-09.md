# Devlog 0: Ryan's script notes (verbatim, pasted 2026-10-09 evening)

Thesis: I'm making the last type of game all conventional game dev advice says a solo dev should make. its online, multiplayer, has depth to it mechanics, but it also has depth in its content.

Title
 1. Breaking every rule of solo game dev at once
 2. I'm making the one game every solo dev is told not to make

Script:

either the hook is like this, where i just suggest what i say, or i go get tons of clips of famous channels saying this like chris etc.

Hook: If you ask any experienced dev what type of game to make as a solo dev, they'll all say the same things "Keep the scope small" "make something single player for your first game" "keep the mechanics simple".

so i, as a first time game devloper whos only real skill is programming, took all of that advice to heart. I then proceeded ot ignore all of it, because the game i want to make cant be done in those bounds.

Intro: I'm neotechnites, I current work full time as a softweare engineer, I've never publiushed a game on steam, and i'm working on doing that now

why: The idea for this game comes from an old halo reach custom game, usually called duck hunt. the idea of that game was 16 ducks are all running through an opsticle course while someone with view of all of them, shoots at them so they cant complete it. if even one of them gets to the end, the get to kill the sniper and the ducks win. I, never played this game with 16 people, me and my buddies would just play it locally but it was still a ton of fun. everytime you ran out it felt like you were being watched, especially if you were int the front and getthing through every uncovered section felt like a win

This idea and concept to me, felt like it could sustain a full game, and be completely fleshed out, and still no one has. the same way prop hunt became witches whatever and mecca camleon, or how TTT became among us.

This idea, has been in my head for a year and a half, a game that explores this concept more thoroughly, and all the things about it that can make it fun.

premise: PANOPTICON is a first-person asymmetric multiplayer game about escaping a prison built so one guard can see everyone. One friend sits in the tower with a sniper rifle; everyone else runs the ring for the exit, and the other prisoners aren't your team. You sprint between cover, shove each other into the line of fire, and whoever makes it out takes the rifle and the tower.

(Ryan on ordering: hook -> intro -> origin -> premise, not concept-first.)

Next (unplaced): explain how im making this game, because ive always wanted to make a game, and the concept for this game while its not my dream game, kind of is in the respect that the idea has been knocking around my head for the last year and a half, and i cant get it out, and the reason im actually making it, and ignoring the advice of all the indie creators, is because i want a game under my belt, i want to be able to play exactly this game with my friends, for others to be able to play it for anyone who doesnt know how fun halo reach duck hunt is, because halo reach duck hunt was so very limited in the ideas it could explore, and i want those ideas to be expanded upon, and because i think indie multiplayer games have gotten lazy and crazy shallow, and i want to prove that a game can be made to have the fun of friendslop, but with the depth, unlocks, and replayability of an era gone by. the times where you had to play 1000 matches to even unlock mr game and watch, or you could keep playing nuketown again and again and again with your friends, swapping up the meta everytime, or the times when every map played somewhat differently.

Ryan's own breakdown: origin (duck hunt) / how to implement (online, unlockable content, depth in mechanics and content) / why that way (friendslop lacks it) / why make a game at all (one under my belt, push friendslop the right direction, a game i actually want to play).

## Script draft, continued (Ryan, pasted later 2026-10-09 evening, verbatim)

This idea and concept to me, felt like it could sustain a full game, and be completely fleshed out, but no one has. the same way prop hunt became witches whatever and mecca camleon, or how TTT became among us, i wanted halo reach duck hunt to become a full game.

This idea, has been in my head for a year and a half, a game that explores this concept more thoroughly, and all the things about it that can make it fun.

So its a game, where your being watched by someone with power over you, and you cant tell if theyre looking at you. hmmmmmm, that seems a lot like a PANOPTICON

[premise: the pitch]

Now, that sounds like exactly the kind of game I'm about to complain about. A little party friendslop game. You play it for three hours with your friends, you laugh, and you never open it again. And that's the problem. Because I think that's what indie multiplayer has become, and I think it looses that absolute magic of needing to spend real time in a game to play all its content, and unlock all its content. nowadays, all content is available from being opened, and you get through all of it in 3 hours. what happened to playing melee for hours, thinking youve got every character, then all of a sudden (cut to mr game and watch intro) stuff like this was literally magic, youre mind was blown, you went from a game that felt complete to being told theres an entire other character, hes 2d, youve never heard of him, and he plays like a freaking weirdo. I remember me and my brother had melee, and played like crazy, and one day he said "i went to my friends house, and he had this weird little stick figure guy named mr game and watch" and i just straight up thought he was lying to me. i thought he was making something up to trick me like older brothers often do. what happened to playing only nuketown with youre buddies, where every round everybody wants to change something about their loadout, just to get the upper hand on one strategy the other player is implementing, just for that to be nullified next round when everybody swaps their loadouts again. what happened to finding out that in an entire single player experience game, it had 10 mini games in it, and one of those minigames has an entire airplane physics engine for some monkeys in balls, multiple power ups, multiple maps, and a wind system. The chao garden was an entire game that you could only get to inside the levels of the main game. This stuff, this is magic of a bygone era that i want my game to feel like. I want to combine the laugh out loud moments of the friendslop, with the depth of games that came out when every game had to be worth youre money and replayable.

# Mid-size indie multiplayer comps: sales vs creator coverage
Date: 2026-10-08. Done for PANOPTICON ($7, friends-only, 1 sniper vs many runners).

## Method and caveats (read first)
- **Sales are ESTIMATES.** Gamalytic was not usable: its pages render with JavaScript and its API needs a paid key. Sources used instead: Raijin.gg (medium-confidence model), SteamSpy owner ranges (often inflated), and the Boxleiter rule (all-language Steam reviews x30 to x50). Review counts come from the Steam appreviews API, pulled 2026-10-08.
- **Price** is the Steam US base price or SteamSpy `initialprice`. Day-one launch price is only confirmed where noted. Wayback was unreachable.
- **Biggest creator** was found by YouTube search (sorted by views) for the game plus about 15 big-creator names. Twitch was checked through articles, not searched exhaustively. Creator size is the subscriber or follower count from Wikipedia, Social Blade or the channel/watch page as of 2026.
- **Spike evidence** is SteamCharts monthly peak concurrent players lined up against video dates. That shows timing only, not cause, and it measures players, not sales.
- "Even Crawl" was taken to be **Crawl** (Powerhoof, 2017). It is asymmetric one-vs-many, but mainly local multiplayer.
- Tier: 10k to 500k copies. Lockdown Protocol is included as an upper-edge row because it is about 0.6M to 1M. Out-of-tier titles that were screened are listed at the bottom.

## Synthesis
- **The biggest creator is usually huge, and it doesn't predict sales.** 15 of the 19 games with an identified creator were played by a channel of 4M+ subscribers. The common ones were Markiplier 38.9M (4 games), Vanoss 26M (4 games) and PewDiePie 109M. Even so, Screencheat (PewDiePie) and Heave Ho (a 20.5M-view Markiplier video) sold only about 40-130K. A mega-creator one-off is normal for this tier, not a ticket out of it.
- **One-off big videos almost never show up on the player charts.** That was true for Screencheat, Boomerang Fu, Panicore, Super Bunny Man, Ship of Fools, Forewarned (Vanoss) and Lovers. Only Heave Ho matches, and Remote Play Together launched at the same time.
- **The real jumps came from a cluster of repeat coverage, or from a mid-size specialist.** Lockdown Protocol went from 105 to 9.3K peak and Nuclear Nightmare from 362 to 10.4K, both in the month a SMii7Yplus video landed. SMii7Yplus has only 4.55M subscribers, but his friend-group-chaos videos pull 7-9M views, and he posted repeat episodes. He also appears on Murky Divers, Dale & Dawson, Forewarned and Super Battle Golf. He is the most predictive single creator in this genre.
- **Breakouts at launch came from many streamers at once.** Super Battle Golf had a 12-streamer shroud lobby, Sidemen and SSundee, peaking at 88K Twitch viewers. Midnight Ghost Hunt had about 160 channels and 66K viewers on day one, led by xQc and forsen. Many mid-size streamers in the same week did more than one large creator later.
- **Launch price pattern:** the 2024-26 hits cluster at $6.99-$9.99: Nuclear Nightmare $6.99, Dale & Dawson $7.99, Murky Divers $8.99, Lockdown and Panicore $9.99, and Mage Arena at $2.99. Older titles priced at $14.99-$24.99 generally sold 50-300K. **$7 for Panopticon is squarely in the price band of the recent breakouts.**
- **Genre and format predict more than creator size.** The recent risers are proximity-chat social deduction, hide-and-seek horror or co-op chaos games that produce clippable moments with 4-8 friends. Sniper vs runners fits that, and it is close to SpyParty. SpyParty was a $24.99, skill-heavy game that topped out around 50-75K, with dunkey as its biggest creator.
- **Other channels can carry a game:** TikTok drove Mage Arena past the tier, and Turkish/Korean regional streamers apparently made Feign about $1M. A free giveaway (Murderous Pursuits, 144K concurrent players) adds owners but not paid sales.
- **Caveat:** all spike reads are monthly SteamCharts timing. No developer postmortem in this set credits a specific creator with sales.

# Breakout series devlogs from unknown creators (researched 2026-10-12)

Second pass for pod decision 377. Companion to breakout-devlogs-2026-10-12.md (no repeats of those 15). Scope here: an episode of a numbered or ongoing devlog series on ONE game, not a standalone single-idea video. 10 videos, 10 channels. Views as of 2026-10-12.

## Method
- Same as pass 1. YouTube search sorted by view count (`results?search_query=...&sp=CAM%253D`) over about 200 new queries (devlog 1 to 12, `devlog #N`, engine-specific, genre-specific, "first devlog", "dev diary"). About 1,700 results; those with 120k+ views and a series-style title (devlog, dev diary, #N, episode, part N) were checked per channel.
- For each channel I pulled the "Oldest" upload order from YouTube's browse API and checked where the breakout episode sits and what earlier uploads got. "Unknown at upload" is an inference from that list, not a sourced subscriber count, except where noted (video 7 has a creator-stated count).
- Subs shown are today's, from the channel page. Subs at upload are not sourced for any video except video 7.
- Caveats: the "Oldest" list is capped at about 30 uploads, so episode counts for long channels are lower bounds. Shorts, deleted and unlisted videos are not visible. Earlier uploads' views are today's, so they may have grown after the breakout (the inference is conservative). Where a creator already had an audience on another platform (stated in description), it is flagged.
- Transcripts are YouTube auto-captions via yt-dlp. Two videos (AdrianSGms, Bisht) have no captions.
- Excluded from this pass: ThinMatrix, Randy (randyprime), DevDuck, Codeer, BenBonk (per instruction), and channels already big at upload (Dani, Domango, Games of Vaibhav, Jess::codes, shawcat and similar). Under the 300k bar: video 10 (244k).

Evidence grades: STRONG = first upload, or all earlier uploads under about 35k views, or a creator-stated tiny sub count. MEDIUM = early in the channel with earlier uploads up to about 80k, or the series itself was already known.

## Common patterns (series format)
1. The breakout is almost always the first episode: #0, 00 or #1 for 8 of 10 (BigHoles, advancenine, Vedinad, InventorDev, Cyzeck, UkDarky, jamessimo, Bob's Mud Hut). The others are #2 (AdrianSGms, Bisht). The first episode carries the pitch and the maker's intro, and later episodes drop sharply: BigHoles 2.2M to 532k to 382k, advancenine 696k to 154k to 123k, InventorDev 1.28M to 173k to 99k, Cyzeck 354k to 67k, Bob 244k to 126k to 36k, jamessimo 352k to 113k to 72k. AdrianSGms and Bisht are the exceptions with later spikes.
2. Episode 1 opens with a one-line premise plus who the maker is: "Hi, I'm Ryan... electrical engineer turned programmer" (advancenine), "Hi, I'm James. I want to build..." (jamessimo), "welcome to my first devlog" (Bob). advancenine has an "About me" chapter (0:19) and UkDarky opens with a recap.
3. A known property or genre makes the title legible: Card Wars, Shadow Fight 2, Call of Duty, Plants vs Zombies, Vampire Survivors, SimCity/Cities: Skylines, Breath of the Wild/Braid. 5 of 10 titles name a recognisable game (Card Wars, Shadow Fight 2, Call of Duty, Plants vs Zombies, Vampire Survivors); SimCity and Skylines are named in the first 30 s of video 8.
4. Thumbnails: one big text claim or ep number on a clean image ("CREATING REAL CARD WARS 00", "10,000 ENEMIES!", "MEDIEVAL!", "Making City! #2", "THE BIRTH OF BOB", "MAKING MY FIRST OPEN WORLD GAME") or a before/after silhouette swap (InventorDev). Counter-example: AdrianSGms is a raw screenshot with no text.
5. Length is 7 to 13 minutes for 8 of 10; the outliers are the 1:38 PvZ clip and the 22-minute jamessimo explainer.
6. Format of an episode: one problem solved on screen at a time (terrain, water, hit animation, parcel algorithm, a bug), ending with "next time". Several state it is a series up front (InventorDev "this whole project is going to be a series", BigHoles "If this video is popular I will try to get another one", Bob "Subscribe if you are interested in seeing the next video").
7. Unknown-at-upload evidence is weaker here than for standalone videos. Only 6 of 10 are STRONG, and only UkDarky has a creator-stated sub count (about 490 to 514). All of these are inferences from upload lists; no creator explained why it worked.

# PANOPTICON devlog 0 timeline: 2026-09-09 to 2026-10-09

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| 2026-09-09 | Built the game from scratch in one day: a greybox ring and tower with a Quake-style controller and a sightline probe, a one-shot rifle, and a first playable win-or-lose round. |
| 2026-09-10 | The hell level got built out for real: a carved stone ring and pit, a glowing portal finish line, and a hollow tower with working traps; runners finally got a visible body. |
| 2026-09-11 | Biggest day yet: real online multiplayer went live with two players syncing a full round, plus a giant swap of creature and tower concepts; an early solid-rock tower design was rejected outright. |
| 2026-09-12 | The lava river section got rebuilt through nine design passes in one day; an entire later redesign was thrown out and reverted back to the simpler version players liked. |
| 2026-09-13 | The three obstacle sections of the hell level — a stalactite cave, a lava-river chain of platforms, and a demon-pad lava run — were carved into the real level geometry. |
| 2026-09-14 | The bots were rebuilt to work on any map without special-casing, and a whole hub world with ten map stalls around the tower went in, alongside new netcode and a growing devlog toolkit. |
| 2026-09-15 | A second and third level, a marble rotunda and a forest clearing, both stood up for the first time, so three different maps now exist side by side in the hub. |
| 2026-09-16 | A performance review across all three maps found triangle count wasn't the real cost; a bug was caught where the runner's own camera was filming inside the tower by mistake. |
| 2026-09-17 | The guard's shot became an actual slow-moving bullet you can see and follow, sitting alongside the old instant-hit option, and both the marble and forest lanes got proper obstacle courses. |
| 2026-09-18 | The hell level's minefield section was built: rows of demon pads and lava cracks cut into the floor, dividing the lap into clear named stretches for the first time. |

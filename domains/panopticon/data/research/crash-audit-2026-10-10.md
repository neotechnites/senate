# PANOPTICON crash audit, 2026-10-10

Base: main 4d7a990f. Branch: crash-audit. Scope: scripts/ (tools/capture out). Six read-only sweeps (net+hub, match+tower+maps, player+camera+optics, weapon+fx+ring, ui+audio+debug, bot+harness), then HIGHs fixed.

Grading note: in a release export GDScript's freed-instance checks are compiled out, so a method call on a freed Object is a real use-after-free (0xc0000005, no log). Any freed-body / freed-rifle path counts as native-crash-capable.

Classes: 1 use-after-free, 2 physics/ragdoll, 3 scene change, 4 audio, 5 multiplayer, 6 runner powers.

Counts: HIGH 3 (UAF 1, physics 1, multiplayer 1); MED 32 (UAF 12, physics 3, scene 4, audio 2, multiplayer 8, powers 3); LOW 41 (UAF 23, physics 6, scene 5, audio 1, multiplayer 5, cross-region 1). No HIGH found in ui/audio/debug, bot/harness, weapon/fx/ring (Rifle static hit ring is is_instance_valid-guarded at every read). Runner-power lifecycle items are MED and left to the powers-crash builder.

## HIGH (3, all fixed on crash-audit)

1. [2] scripts/player/death_ragdoll.gd:292-301 via prisoner_avatar.gd `_try_ragdoll` / `throw_out` -- the killed client's own body is the viewed body, so FirstPersonHead modifiers hold Head, Spine and arms at 0.001 scale; `physical_bones_start_simulation()` then creates parts from collapsed, scaled poses (degenerate bodies, joint frames authored at full scale): a NaN/explosion source inside the physics server with no GDScript error. Matches "host kills client". Plausible, not proven. Fix: `PrisonerAvatar._restore_hidden_bones()` un-hides every hider (rest scale) immediately before both `_ragdoll.start(...)` calls.
2. [1] scripts/match/match_controller.gd `_build_participants` (queue_free of bot bodies) and net_match.gd `rebuild_hub` (frees every runner_container child) -- the single `controller.rifle` is reparented onto the holder's Head; when a bot holds it and the roster is rebuilt, the rifle is freed with the body while `controller.rifle != null` stays true; every later `rifle.` call is a release-only native crash. Fix: `_stow_rifle()` before the frees in `_build_participants`, and at the top of `release_roster()` (net_match calls it right after queueing the bodies).
3. [5] scripts/net/net_match.gd `_start_now` -- a client that drops while LAUNCHING turns its seat BOT before `_started`, so `_on_seat_occupancy_changed` skips it; the body stays HUMAN on a dead RemoteIntentSource for the whole match (softlock if it was the guard). Fix: after `controller.start_match()`, re-run the handover for every seat that is now a bot but whose link still has an owner peer.

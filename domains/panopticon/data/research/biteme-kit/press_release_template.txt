Steam Announcement Kit - Press Release Template


Press Release 
Whilst we will also be doing directed press outreach, a press release is a low-effort way to broadcast to press outlets that you have announced your game. Outlets that may not have the same reach as the ones in the Press & Media Contacts spreadsheet, but can still have an impact on your game’s social proof and visibility. 
Press releases get distributed through gamespress.com. You are not required to create an account, this is only needed if you as a developer want to see other developers’ press releases.
You submit your press release by sending an email to inbox@gamespress.com.
The email should contain a filled in version of the template below, and no further information.  The embargo statement (even if for immediate release) should always be bold and in red at the top of the email. If you are stating a timed embargo, always include the timezone as well.
When adding URLs to your press release, always use the full URL, instead of an embed like this. This way your press release can easily be copy-pasted without losing data. 
Do not attach files such as screenshots, these should always be pulled from your press kit.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE 
A desktop idler you can play with friends: “Desktop Island: Idle Together!” announced for Steam
Antwerp, Belgium - August 7, 2026 - Independent Belgian developer BiteMe Games announced their co-op desktop idler life sim, Desktop Island: Idle Together! today. The Steam page is live now and the game launches in December 2026.
Desktop Island is a life sim that lives on your desktop. Play it fullscreen and hands-on, or park it at the bottom of your screen, in a floating window, or as notifications only, and let the island tick along while you work. Restore it solo or with up to five friends: choose between 10+ professions to master, from woodcutting and fishing to animal care and farming, upgrade the community hall, unlock new buildings and customise your village.
Idle games are usually a solo affair, and co-op life sims usually depend on one player hosting the save. Desktop Island does neither. Changes sync to a shared island automatically, so anyone can drop in whenever they want, even if the original host isn't online.
Announcement Trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Lx8kgpWeWg
Key Features
•      Active life sim or idle incremental playstyle
•      Four display styles: fullscreen, bottom of screen, floating window, notification only
•      Co-op for up to five players, with no host required
•      10+ professions to master
•      Cozy, Animal Crossing-inspired soundtrack
•      Localised into 16 languages
Availability
Desktop Island: Idle Together! will launch on Steam in December 2026. A playtest is running now, and access can be requested from the Steam page.

Steam Announcement Kit - Press outreach template


Press Outreach 
Alongside with a general broadcast of your game’s announcement press release, you also want to contact individual press & media outlets to increase your chance of coverage.
These emails shouldn’t be copy-pastes of your press release, but should instead read as a more human version, similar to the format you would later on send to content creators as well.


I’ve compiled my own list of press & media outlets, which can be found here:
Press & Media Contacts


This is an international selection of media outlets, and it’s generally advised to localize your emails for the language of the publication. Write your outreach once in English, and then use a tool to translate it to other languages. This is standard practice for press, so even if it doesn’t sound 100% natural, you will be fine.  


Important to note on the topic of localization, only reach out to an outlet if you are planning on supporting that game’s language. Sending an English-only game to a Korean outlet won’t give them much of a reason to cover your game.

Press Outreach Template
Replace the elements in [BRACKETS] with your own game/studio information.
The only exception is the subject’s [Announcement], keep this in brackets so the marketing beat is clear.
________________




Subject: [Announcement] [GAME NAME] - [GENRE / WHAT’S SPECIAL]

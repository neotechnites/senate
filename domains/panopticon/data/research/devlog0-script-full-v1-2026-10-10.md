# Devlog 0 script, full draft v1 (Ryan, verbatim, 2026-10-10)

Hook: If you ask any experienced dev what type of game to make as a solo dev, they'll all say the same things "Keep the scope small" "make something single player for your first game" "keep the mechanics simple". 

so i, as a first time game devloper whos only real skill is programming, took all of that advice to heart. I then proceeded ot ignore all of it,  Because the game I want to make is online, it's multiplayer, and the part they'd really hate, i want it to have depth. Not three hours of fun and uninstall. actual depth.

Intro: I'm neotechnites, I current work full time as a softweare engineer, I've never publiushed a game on steam, and i'm working on doing that now

why: The idea for my game comes from an old halo reach custom game, usually called duck hunt. the idea of that game was 16 ducks are all running through an opsticle course while someone with view of all of them, shoots at them so they cant complete it. if even one of them gets to the end, thye get to kill the sniper and the ducks win. I, never played this game with 16 people, me and my buddies would just play it locally with at most 4 of us, but it was still a ton of fun. everytime you ran out it felt like you were being watched, especially if you were int the front. and getthing through every uncovered section felt like a win

This idea and concept to me, felt like it could sustain a full game, and be completely fleshed out, but no one has. the same way prop hunt became witches whatever and mecca camleon, or how TTT became among us, i wanted halo reach duck hunt to become a full game.

This idea, has been in my head for a year and a half, a game that explores this concept more thoroughly, and all the things about it that can make it fun. 

So its a game, where your being watched by someone with power over you, and you cant tell if theyre looking at you. hmmmmmm, that seems a lot like a PANOPTICON

[premise, the pitch: PANOPTICON is a first-person asymmetric multiplayer game about escaping a prison built so one guard can see everyone. One friend sits in the tower with a sniper rifle; everyone else runs the ring for the exit, and the other prisoners aren't your team. You sprint between cover, shove each other into the line of fire, and whoever makes it out takes the rifle and the tower.]

Now, that sounds like exactly the kind of game I'm about to complain about. A little party friendslop game. You play it for three hours with your friends, you laugh, and you never open it again. And that's the problem. Because I think that's what indie multiplayer has become, and I think it looses that absolute magic of needing to spend real time in a game to play all its content, and unlock all its content. nowadays, all content is available from being opened, and you get through all of it in 3 hours. what happened to playing melee for hours, thinking youve got every character, then all of a sudden (cut to mr game and watch intro) stuff like this was literally magic, youre mind was blown, you went from a game that felt complete to being told theres an entire other character, hes 2d, youve never heard of him, and he plays like a freaking weirdo. I remember me and my brother had melee, and played like crazy, and one day he said "i went to my friends house, and he had this weird little stick figure guy named mr game and watch" and i just straight up thought he was lying to me. i thought he was making something up to trick me like older brothers often do. what happened to playing only nuketown with youre buddies, where every round everybody wants to change something about their loadout, just to get the upper hand on one strategy the other player is implementing, just for that to be nullified next round when everybody swaps their loadouts again. what happened to finding out that in an entire single player experience game, it had 10 mini games in it, and one of those minigames has an entire airplane physics engine for some monkeys in balls, multiple power ups, multiple maps, and a wind system. The chao garden was an entire game that you could only get to inside the levels of the main game. This stuff, this is magic of a bygone era that i want my game to feel like. I want to combine the laugh out loud moments of the friendslop, with the depth of games that came out when every game had to be worth youre money and replayable.

Now, theres a reason why every indie dev who knows what theyre talking about says not to do this. Online multiplayer, means netcode. depth and unlockable, means content, and content, takes time, and well... (shot of some really shitty map or something ive made) These are the art skills im working with

But i cant let this game go unmade, so i decided im gunna be the guy to make it, and if im eating up a game idea as sacred and beloved as halo reach custom game lobby duck hunt, then im going to have to do it justice.

So whats this actually gunna look like? well, let me show you what ive got so far, all of it is subject to change, consdiering im stil early in development, but this is a development log, so lets actually log some development

The first thing i did was try to put together a rough mock up of what this game was going to be, so i made a donut as the ring, so that wherever the sniper viewed them from was about the same distance of shot, and put a platform in the middle for the sniper to stand on. then, i added movement and let everyone move around. Then a very basic hit scan weapon, and round system. and bots that just go around in a circle (show some sniper pov gameplay) all of this didnt take all that long, and having a prototype up quick, and lots of game devs know, was quite motivating

Now that i have the very very most basic version of this game, I wanted to start making some actual models for it. For the player, i came up with this little scraggly guy, with a big head. i wanted the players to be something humanoid, but not just humans. To anmiate them, i just got some free animations online. then, i tried modeling the tower for the sniper to be in, which, i atctually didnt hate to much. The consept for the first map is a panopticon, in hell, in a deep deep pit. I got the idea and look from the adventure time episode return to the nightospeher, where finn and jake wake up in this prison thats a pit in the nightsopehre. so far, this is kind of the hero map for my game. then, i modeled the actual map, a bit pit, with cells in the walls, and ring for the players to actually run around. then i modeled some simple elements and placed them around, somewhat haphazardly, just so it wasnt a flat donut. 

Now was the moment of truth, it was time to test it with a real human person. so, i got on a call with my buddy, and we tested it, and low an behold. it all worked, first try. the rounds worked, the map looked awesome, it was fun, the design was perfect, and not one networking bug. Ok, obviously, thats not what happened at all. So, first, when he booted it up, the basic menu i steup for someone ot host and someone else to join, didnt fit in his view right, it was too big, and he wasnt even able to click the buttons he needed to to join my match. so, i fixed that, and sent him a new build. now, this time, we were actually able to get in a match together, and, we were able to see each other, and run around, then we actually tried playing a match. and, lets say it, it didnt go completely smoothly. the build I'd sent him didn't have a rifle in it. Not his, not mine. The exporter had quietly dropped the gun out of the match scene when it packed the build, so we were two guys in a prison with nothing to shoot each other with. Once that was fixerd and we did have a sniper, when I died, he didn't see me die. He saw me fall over face first and then just… lie there, face-planted, for the rest of the round. On my screen I was dead and watching the death camera. On his, I was a guy taking a nap on the ground. The bodies I was sending him had no idea whether they were on the ground or in the air, so they never left the death pose. when he died, nothing happened. No death screen, no death camera, no footsteps the whole time either, and if he hit Play Again it wiped his match while mine kept going. The last thing before we could actually play rounds, was when he reached the portal that was supposed to make you the sniper for the next round, nothing happened. The point, is i learned very quickly why the knowledgable, experienced, good game devs give the advice they do. However, i was able to get it to a point of playable, and while it looks ugly, the mechanics are currently janky, and nothing is properly designed to be fun, we did still manage to have fun playing it. there were a couple of rounds, that almost, just a bit, felt like a fun game i would actually want to play, and that was enough for me to feel validated in this idea, and this porject.

So thats where im at with the project, and the most valuable thing for a game dev is feedback, and all of it is welcome. What type of stuff would you want to see in the game? Are there any mechanics or map themes that you think would improve it? What about traps on the map for the runners that would be fun? i want to hear every single thought and opinion anyone has about my game, because i beleive the best way to make a game fun for players, is to just ask what they think is fun

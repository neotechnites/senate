# CC music like "SR20DET" (Blksmiith): trailer candidates
Date: 2026-10-05

**What was asked for:** free tracks licensed CC BY, CC0 or CC BY-SA (no NC, no ND) that sound like modern SoundCloud atmospheric breakcore / dark DnB. Shape wanted: drums from 0:00, a hard cut, then a drop with much louder bass. SR20DET itself (all-rights-reserved): cut at 5.4–5.5 s, then +6 dB overall and +20 dB bass.

**Bottom line:** this scene almost never uses commercial CC. The artists themselves keep their tracks all-rights-reserved or CC BY-NC-SA. I crawled SR20DET's related tracks two hops out (933 tracks, 641 artists) and found zero tracks with a usable licence. No CC track matches the SR20DET shape at ~5 s. The best ones have the same structure (drums, a hard cut, then a drop with much louder bass) at 9–21 s. The trailer edit can start a few seconds before the cut.

**Method:** SoundCloud api-v2 searches with filter.license=to_use_commercially or to_modify_commercially, covering about 75 scene terms plus tag pages for breakcore, dnb, jungle, atmospheric, halftime and breakbeat (about 6,200 CC tracks). I also walked SR20DET's related tracks and the catalogues of the artists found there. After dropping remixes, bootlegs, labels, mixes, AI patterns and anything before 2019, 812 tracks were left. I analysed the first 60 s of each, then the full track for the shortlist. Measurements: the size of the cut (dB), the overall and bass (25–120 Hz) gain after it, when the drums start, and how close the spectrum is to SR20DET's post-drop section. I re-checked the licence on each track itself and confirmed every link through SoundCloud oEmbed (all returned 200).

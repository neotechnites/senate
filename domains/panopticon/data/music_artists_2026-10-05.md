## What it costs (sources)

1. Itch.io forum, kyro: $65 per loop track, $130 per full track, 100% upfront. https://itch.io/t/6676109/jungle-breakcorednb-music-fast-punchy-high-energy-tracks-for-your-game
2. Itch.io forum, WWG: $20 per jungle DnB song. https://itch.io/t/5187870/jungle-music-and-game-music-fl-studio-game-producer-game-director-game-writer
3. Game Developer, "How to Commission Music for Your Game": hobbyist $30-$100, independent $200-$400, studios over $1,000 per minute. https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-to-commission-music-for-your-game
4. Bugnet: $50 to several hundred dollars per finished minute; "$300/minute with full game-and-OST rights and two revisions can be cheaper than $100/minute with none defined." https://bugnet.io/blog/how-much-does-game-music-cost
5. Artfolio: original scores EUR 300-1,200 per finished minute vs EUR 20-300 per track for royalty-free packs; license tiers non-exclusive (reuse after 24-month embargo) < exclusive (studio owns game rights, composer keeps soundtrack distribution and PRO royalties) < full buy-out ("higher fee", no percentage given). https://www.artfolio.com/article/pitching-original-scores-for-indie-games-timelines-asset-packs-and-license-fees
6. GameSoundCon: composer range $250-$2,500 per minute; example anchor "$1,000/minute for a full buyout" discounted for tight budgets. https://www.gamesoundcon.com/post/2016/03/11/becoming-a-game-music-composer-what-should-i-charge-for-indie-games

Fiverr listings for DnB game music reportedly $20-$50 per track (search-snippet only; Fiverr pages returned 403, UNCONFIRMED).

Reading: at this tier, track-level prices are $20-$130 per track for non-exclusive/loosely-scoped work; honest exclusive/buy-out work runs roughly $200-$400 per finished minute (about $600-$1,500 for a 3-4 minute track). No source gives a hard exclusivity percentage; plan on roughly 1.5-3x the base rate as an assumption, not a data point. For a 45s trailer cue the quote is likely a flat fee, probably $100-$300.

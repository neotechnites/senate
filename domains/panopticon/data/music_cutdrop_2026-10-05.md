Method: SoundCloud public streams (Zorrovian via his Bandcamp streams, since his SoundCloud audio is DRM-locked), analysed privately and deleted. Cut = full-band level falls >= 12 dB for 0.15-3 s (or drums stop with >= 6 dB dip) after a sustained drum section; drop = level back to >= the pre-cut level within 3 s with a strong bass onset. Kept only hits where the drop is bigger than what came before (level +3 dB or bass +4 dB), which removes ordinary break chops. Times are mm:ss.ss. Cut depth = dB below the pre-cut level.

# Music artists: who replies, and who allows use of their music (2026-10-05)

**Method.** I used SoundCloud's public api-v2 with the client_id from soundcloud.com's page scripts. For each artist I pulled every track, then the threaded comments on all tracks that have comments, or on the 40 most-commented tracks if there were more. I kept the comments posted under the artist's own user id and matched each one to the comment it answers, using the @mention and the thread. I also scanned bios, track descriptions and the license field on every track (all tracks, not just the 40).

**What was scanned:** YET 34/34 tracks with comments · MIL3SPERHOUR 27/27 · Shiny 40/63 · aak3 40/93 · ALFAXYSM 14/14 · System ST91 40/93 · arcologies 40/162 · Zorrovian 40/52 · Pizza Hotline 40/61.

**Caveat.** "Parent deleted" means the comment the artist answered no longer exists. A question with no public reply may still have been answered by DM.

## Ranking (most promising to contact first)

1. **Pizza Hotline.** The bio asks for this kind of request directly: "For compostion, remixes, or collabs then call the hotline: pizzahotline247 [at] gmail [dot] com". He calls himself a "VIDEO GAME COMPOSER". He makes few public replies, so use the email.
2. **arcologies.** He replies more than anyone here: 114 replies, the latest on 2026-08-28. He gives away codes, stems and project files, and 43 tracks are under a Creative Commons license that allows commercial use. Counterpoint: he did not publicly answer a direct game-licensing question.
3. **MIL3SPERHOUR.** The bio has a contact email. He replied "sure! go ahead" to someone asking to use a track in a mix, and "holy crap i would be so down" to a collab ask. Counterpoints: a game studio's offer to buy tracks got no public reply, and his CC tracks are non-commercial only.
4. **System ST91.** He writes long answers to questions, sells on Bandcamp, has made a fictional game OST, and says he has made music "commercially" since 2018. Counterpoint: "am i allowed to use for a project?, ill give credit" went unanswered.
5. **Shiny.** This is the only explicit permission among the nine, but it came with the condition "as long as you don't make money of it". Shiny has made only 5 replies, the latest in 2024-12. One track is under an exclusive license to a label (Ictasis).
6. **YET soundsystem.** Very active (latest reply 2026-09-19), but all 45 replies are thank-yous. Nobody has asked about using the music, and the bio has no contact details.
7. **Zorrovian.** The bio has a bookings email. Most uploads are DJ mixes of other people's tracks, so the CC licenses on those mean nothing for use. Little original music is public yet.
8. **aak3.** 29 replies, the latest on 2025-03-16, all small talk. There are no signals about sharing or business and no contact in the bio.
9. **ALFAXYSM.** Zero replies on any track. The bio link alfaxysm.net shows "This site is currently suspended."
